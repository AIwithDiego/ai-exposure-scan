Design tokens for AI Exposure Scan

Two layers. The severity layer (red, amber, slate and grey) is unchanged from v0.2. A separate brand layer in violet and teal gives the dashboard its identity, and it never uses red or amber. Every component below uses the class names that dashboard/src/App.jsx already renders, so applying it is mostly a stylesheet swap.

  • Brand palette: violet #6d28d9 / #8b5cf6 and teal #0d9488 / #2dd4bf. The accent is violet: buttons, links, the fix-box rule and focus.
  • Header band (above): a full-width violet → teal gradient with the hexagon logo, in CSS only. White text reaches ≥ 5.4:1 on every stop.
  • Page #faf8ff with white cards separated by a soft shadow and a 1px #e4e0f0 border. No dark grey borders.
  • Score: a 96px ring on the site view, with the arc in the band colour. The overview chip is bigger and bolder.
  • Stat cards: an icon per type on a violet or teal tile, with a big tabular number.
  • Unchanged: severity pills, the neutral "Couldn't scan" banner, EU tags and bars.

00Principles

Owners of small sites read this dashboard, often on a shared screen or a projector. It should be calm, legible from the back of the room, and never shout more than the finding deserves.

Red means danger, only
  • Red is used for high severity and poor scores, nothing else.
  • Brand colours (violet, teal) never carry meaning about risk. Actions, links, focus and the active nav item are violet.
Urgency = darkness
  • Severity fills get lighter as urgency drops, so the order still reads in greyscale or on a washed-out projector.
Evidence is literal
  • URLs and snippets are always monospace on an inset surface, so it is clear what was actually seen.
Soft edges
  • White cards on #faf8ff, separated by a violet-tinted shadow and a 1px #e4e0f0 border. Radius is 8–10px, panel padding 24px.
  • Only things you can click react to hover.

08Severity pills

A lightness ramp: high is a solid dark red fill with white text, medium a mid amber fill, low a pale slate fill, and info a white outline. Because the fills get lighter as urgency drops, the order still reads in greyscale or on a washed-out projector.
