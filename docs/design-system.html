AI Exposure Scan

A calm dark intelligence console. Teal-black page, glass panels lit from above, a champagne orb as the only warm brand mark.

Design system v0.4 · applied in dashboard/src/styles.css

This document embeds the real dashboard stylesheet: <style id="app-css"> is a copy of dashboard/src/styles.css, so every example below renders exactly as the app does. It has no violet, no purple gradients, no gradient header band, no filled icon tiles and no all-caps labels.

00Principles

Light, not shadow

Surfaces are separated by a 1px edge, an inset top highlight and a luminous top hairline. There are no drop shadows.

Severity is a ramp

The marks get lighter as urgency drops: filled › tinted › outline › dashed for pills, and 3px › 2.5px › 1.5px › 1.5px dashed for the card bracket. The word is always there too.

Champagne is an edge

It is used for the logo orb, the selected tile, row ticks and the site-head edge, and never for text or pill fills. Teal is for focus and ticks.

Quiet type

System font, big numbers at weight 300, headings at 500, no uppercase anywhere.

04Surfaces & radius

Glass is rgba(26,48,51,.74) with backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(140%), falling back to solid #172a2d. Every glass surface gets a 1px edge, an inset rgba(255,255,255,.10) top highlight and a 1px luminous top hairline drawn as a background layer. Behind everything, body::before paints three soft glows: teal top-left at .26, champagne top-right at .13 and deep teal at the bottom at .24. None of them is red or coral.

18Accessibility

Focus

A 2px teal :focus-visible outline, offset 2px. Table rows use an inset outline.

Motion

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce turns off the ring sweep and all transitions.

Forced colours

The glows are hidden. Borders, ticks, dots and brackets switch to CanvasText, and the bars and ring to Highlight.

Contrast

Body text ≥ 7:1, small labels and pills ≥ 4.5:1, measured on the running app (section 02).
